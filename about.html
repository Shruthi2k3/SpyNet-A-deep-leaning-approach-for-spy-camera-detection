About SpyNet

SpyNet is an intelligent, real-time spy camera detection system developed using advanced deep learning and computer vision techniques. With the growing concern over hidden surveillance devices invading personal spaces, SpyNet aims to provide a reliable, AI-powered solution to detect and alert users about unauthorized cameras in their environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect individual privacy in an increasingly connected world. SpyNet is designed to empower users with the ability to monitor their surroundings using their device’s camera feed and detect potential spy devices — all without requiring any specialized equipment.

How It Works

SpyNet uses the power of deep learning models like COCO-SSD, which is capable of detecting common objects including hidden camera components. By analyzing live video streams from the user's camera, the system processes each frame in real-time and highlights suspicious devices that could be disguised as everyday items.

Why SpyNet?

  • ✅ Real-time detection of hidden cameras
  • ✅ Lightweight and accessible via web browser
  • ✅ Designed for both personal and professional use

Our Vision

We envision a safer digital world where people can confidently maintain their privacy without fear of hidden surveillance. SpyNet is a step toward creating that secure environment — blending technology, transparency, and trust.

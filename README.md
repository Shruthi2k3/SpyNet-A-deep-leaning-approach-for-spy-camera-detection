# SpyNet-A-deep-leaning-approach-for-spy-camera-detection

Spynet is a next-generation surveillance detection system focused on preserving user privacy in environments vulnerable to unauthorized monitoring, such as hotels, homestays, changing rooms, and public restrooms. In recent years, the proliferation of ultra-compact and covert spy cameras has raised serious concerns over personal safety and privacy. Traditional detection methods—such as infrared (IR) sensors, RF (Radio Frequency) detectors, and network traffic analyzers—are not only costly and complex but also limited by their dependency on specific types of hardware and transmission patterns. These methods often fail to detect non-transmitting or concealed devices and require technical skills to operate, thereby excluding a large portion of non-expert users.
Spynet addresses these limitations through a purely software-based approach using computer vision and deep learning. At the core of the system lies the COCO-SSD (Single Shot MultiBox Detector) model integrated with a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), which enables the real-time identification of surveillance devices using standard image or video input. This makes Spynet accessible to anyone with a smartphone or webcam, eliminating the need for additional hardware. The system processes live camera feeds and visually highlights suspicious devices, allowing users to scan their surroundings quickly and effectively. With high accuracy, ease of deployment, and platform independence, Spynet serves as an essential tool for empowering individuals to reclaim control over their private spaces. Its lightweight architecture and real-time processing capabilities position it as a practical, scalable, and impactful solution in the ongoing fight for digital privacy.
